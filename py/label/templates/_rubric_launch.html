Relevance rubric

Mark relevant if the press release's primary purpose is to announce a specific product or service that is newly available from the issuing company.

Tiebreaker: after reading this, can I name a specific thing a customer could now buy, sign up for, or download that they couldn't before? If yes → relevant.

Relevant: new product launches; major releases with their own name; public beta / early access (if signups open); new services with defined scope; co-developed products.

Irrelevant: funding / M&A / earnings / hires; awards & analyst rankings; partnerships & integrations; feature updates & patches; regional expansion of existing products; rebrands; clinical/research milestones without a product; events; corporate updates; discontinuations.

Irrelevant w/ product info: not a launch (still trained as a negative), but the release carries substantive detail about an already-launched product — capabilities, specs, pricing, customer wins. Flagged via has_product_details so these can be reused later, and kept in training as hard negatives to curb false positives.

Hard calls: "industry's first" hype doesn't count — apply the tiebreaker. Named & priced thing → new product. Update to a named existing product → irrelevant. Free / open-source releases count if discrete. Security products live under domain software. When genuinely unsure → skip.
